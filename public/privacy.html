Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to Elvion IDE. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Elvion IDE desktop application and authentication services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with your Google Account, we receive basic profile information authorized by you via Google OAuth:

  • Email address: Used exclusively to identify your developer account and manage session state.
  • Basic profile info: Name and avatar URL to display in the IDE interface.
  • Authentication tokens: Securely stored locally on your device to maintain your signed-in state.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is strictly used for:

  • Authenticating your session within Elvion IDE.
  • Routing code completion and AI assistant requests to your selected inference providers (e.g. Groq, Cerebras).
  • Displaying your active user profile and quota state in the IDE.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your data:

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
  • Your code and queries are processed in-flight for AI generation and are not stored or used for model training without your explicit consent.
  • OAuth tokens are transmitted using secure HTTPS / TLS encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

Elvion IDE integrates with Google OAuth for authentication and OpenAI-compatible inference endpoints (such as Groq and Cerebras). Usage of these third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, please contact us at support@elvion.dev.
